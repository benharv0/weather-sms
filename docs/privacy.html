Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Weather Update ("we", "us") operates a small, private daily weather text message service for friends and family.

Information we collect

We collect only your mobile phone number, which you give us yourself when you agree to receive messages. We also keep a record that you opted in.

How we use it

We use your phone number only to send you one weather text message per day and to respond if you reply HELP or STOP. We do not use it for any other purpose.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Your number is shared only with our SMS delivery provider, Twilio, so that messages can be delivered.

Opting out and deleting your data

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. To have your phone number deleted, contact us at bennettharvey05@gmail.com.
